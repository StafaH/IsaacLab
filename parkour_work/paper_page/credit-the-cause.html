Credit the Cause

Evaluation-driven allocation of simulated worlds for humanoid box climbing

A Unitree G1 learns to climb boxes of 0.38 to 1.14 m from public motion clips. Some simulated worlds imitate a tracker, others give one Boolean reward for standing on the box, and a rule that reads evaluation worlds inside the same simulation decides how many of each to run. The policy never observes a clip.

Read these with care. Every result is from simulation, without domain randomisation, with a policy that observes state a robot would have to estimate. The 105-task policy has two seeds and a third is training; its tracker, the even mix and the held-out runs are one seed each. The numbers, what failed and the open decisions are in the results report.
